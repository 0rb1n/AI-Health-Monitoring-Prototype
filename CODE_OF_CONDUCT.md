# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, experience level, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume positive intent and ask clarifying questions.
- Give actionable, kind feedback.
- Keep discussions technical and inclusive.
- Respect privacy, especially around health-related data.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, hate speech, or personal attacks
- Trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments
- Publishing private information without explicit permission
- Repeatedly derailing technical discussions

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards and taking appropriate corrective action for unacceptable behavior.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it to maintainers via the security/contact process in [`SECURITY.md`](./SECURITY.md).

All good-faith reports will be reviewed and handled promptly and fairly.
